# Sand Dig! — Tactile Tunnel Puzzle

A finger-digging sand physics puzzle inspired by **Dig This!** and **Where's My Water?**, designed for ages **4–5**.

- **Controls**: Drag your finger or mouse through the sand to carve tunnels so the smiling marbles roll into the green cup.
- **10 Handcrafted Levels** across Beach, Candy, and Crystal Cave themes with buried bonus stars and solid rock obstacles.
