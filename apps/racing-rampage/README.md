# Racing Rampage 🔥🏎️💥

A 3D arcade vehicular combat and stunt racing game inspired by **Racing Rampage** on Poki.

## Features
- **3D Third-Person Highway Mayhem**: Powered by Three.js (`./three.min.js` bundled locally for zero-dependency offline play) with a 2.5D Canvas fallback.
- **Heavy Arsenal**:
  - **Roof-Mounted Gatling Guns**: Auto-fires tracer rounds at enemy vehicles in your path (toggleable via `F` or HUD button).
  - **Homing Rockets (`Space` / `J`)**: High-explosive missiles with area-of-effect blast damage and airborne **"Death From Above"** stunt bonuses.
  - **Shockwave Bombs (`E` / `B`)**: Detonates a massive blast wave around your rig to clear surrounding traffic.
  - **Nitro Overdrive (`Shift` / `W` / `Up`)**: Ignites twin afterburners for 150%+ speed and 2.6x ramming damage.
- **Stunts, Fuel & Tanker Chain Blasts**:
  - Launch off **Mega Ramps** for airtime cash bonuses and landing shockwaves.
  - Manage your **Fuel Tank (`⛽`)** by grabbing fuel canisters and hitting 1,000m **Checkpoints** across 4 dynamic biomes (*Scorched Canyon*, *Neon Overdrive*, *Toxic Refinery*, *Magma Meltdown*).
  - Blow up volatile **Tanker Trucks** to trigger chain-reaction explosions.
- **Combat Garage & Progression**:
  - Unlock 4 distinct 3D vehicles (*Rust Rebel*, *Viper GT*, *War Hound 4x4*, *Doom Juggernaut*) and upgrade 4 core systems up to Level 8 (persisted in `localStorage`).
