# Bouncy Hoops! — Trick-Shot Basketball

A colorful, physics-based trick-shot basketball game inspired by **Blumgi Bounce**, designed to be fun and accessible for **5-year-olds** (with a **Big Kid Mode** for ages 7+).

## Features
- **One-Button Hold & Release (plus Optional Drag Aim)**: Hold anywhere on screen (or press `Space`) to charge your shot and watch the rainbow dotted trajectory preview bounce off walls, trampolines, and bumpers; release to launch!
- **Kid Mode (5+, Default)**: Helpful rainbow bounce guide, generous hoop rim, and instant unlimited retries with zero frustration.
- **3 Bright Worlds, 15 Handcrafted Levels + Endless Trick Shots**:
  1. Sunny Park (Levels 1–5): Direct swishes, wall arcs, trampolines, bank shots, and bumpers.
  2. Bounce Factory (Levels 6–10): Moving elevator walls, double trampolines, magic portals, moving hoops, and pinball bumpers.
  3. Starry Castle (Levels 11–15): Ceiling rebounds, portal-trampoline combos, twin gates, and moving castle hoops.
- **6 Unlockable Bouncy Buddies**: Sunny Slime, Bouncy Frog, Happy Kitty, Dino Egg, Star Alien, and Rainbow Puff.
