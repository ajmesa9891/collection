# Buddy Rush! — 3D Crowd Adventure

A bright, kid-friendly 3D crowd-runner game inspired by *Count Control Legends* and *Count Masters*, designed specifically for ages **4–5** (with a **Big Kid Mode** for ages 6+).

## Features
- **3D WebGL Engine (Three.js)**: Smooth 60fps 3D characters, shadows, particle confetti bursts, translucent glowing gates, and dynamic camera zoom — zero build step, works offline.
- **Kid-Friendly Counting Gates**:
  - **Little Explorer Mode (Ages 4–5, Default)**: Small friendly numbers (`+1` to `+6`, `×2` Golden Doubler, `-1` to `-3`) paired with **visual counting dots** and color-coding so pre-readers can easily see which gate grows their team! Includes a one-time **Fairy Rescue** per level.
  - **Big Kid Mode (Ages 6+)**: Faster speed, bigger arithmetic numbers, and tougher bosses.
- **5 Themed Worlds, 15 Handcrafted Levels + Endless Rainbow Mode**:
  1. Sunny Meadow (Levels 1–3)
  2. Candy Kingdom (Levels 4–6)
  3. Dino Jungle (Levels 7–9)
  4. Frozen Frostland (Levels 10–12)
  5. Starry Space (Levels 13–15)
- **Boss Battles & Rainbow Bonus Stairs**: Swarm-boop giant silly bosses & toy castles, then march surviving buddies up the `×1`–`×5` Rainbow Staircase for multiplied Golden Stars!
- **Upgrades & 5 Unlockable 3D Buddy Teams**: Blue Bots, Sunny Duckies, Dino Pals, Star Bunnies, and Rainbow Bears.

## Controls
- **Touch / Mouse**: Slide left & right anywhere on the screen.
- **Keyboard**: Left/Right Arrow keys or `A` / `D`.
