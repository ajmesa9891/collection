# Pizza Party! — Kids Cooking Kitchen

A bright, zero-reading visual cooking game inspired by **Papa's Pizzeria** and **Yummy Taco**, designed for ages **4–5**.

- **Visual Order Bubbles**: Cute animal friends show picture icons of the sauce and toppings they want.
- **Interactive Kitchen**: Spread Tomato, Cheese, or Berry sauce, sprinkle toppings, bake until the oven dings, and serve!
- **Orders Mode & Free-Play Chef Mode**: Progress through days or switch to Free-Play to make silly rainbow pizzas anytime.
